Delivered and supported [open data training](https://urbantide.com/output/opendata) for Irish public bodies with UrbanTide, under the [DPER training framework](https://data.gov.ie/pages/trainingsupportforpublicbodies) for Ireland’s Open Data Initiative—from classroom sessions through to online delivery after 2020, with courses continuing into 2025.

Courses delivered individually or as part of the trainer team:

- General Introduction to Open Data
- Training on the Open Data Directive
- Training on Data Audits and Data Publication
- Training on Data Analytics and Visualisations
- Training on Data Anonymisation Techniques

The programme helps public sector staff meet open-data obligations under the [Open Data and reuse of PSI Directive](https://data.gov.ie/pages/trainingsupportforpublicbodies). See [UrbanTide’s case study](https://urbantide.com/output/opendata) and [four-year retrospective](https://urbantide.com/output/dper-training-news).
