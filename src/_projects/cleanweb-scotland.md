Founded and ran Cleanweb Scotland meetups from 2013 to 2017. It brought together technologists, entrepreneurs, and sustainability advocates to explore how digital tools can support climate and environmental goals.

For an overview, this article by Grist looks at the [cleanweb](https://grist.org/science/can-something-called-the-cleanweb-help-fight-climate-change/) movement, and how it was a forum for discussing the internet and connected systems as infrastructure for sustainability, alongside the hardware of cleantech, through apps, open data, sensor networks, and new web-based business models.
