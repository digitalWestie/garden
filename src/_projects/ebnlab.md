Full-stack development of [EBNLab](https://ebnlab.com/)—software that helps innovation teams surface and rank real needs (from stakeholders and customers) before generating ideas, so effort goes to problems worth solving. The [workflow](https://ebnlab.com/what-we-do) covers structured capture, validation, and prioritisation, then clearer, evidence-backed ideation.
