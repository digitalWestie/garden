Managed and led full-stack development of [EBNLab](https://ebnlab.com/) for InsightInnovate Ltd. - software that helps innovation and R&D teams surface and rank real needs (from stakeholders and customers) before generating ideas, in line with the ISO 56002 Innovation Management System.

The [workflow](https://ebnlab.com/what-we-do) covers structured capture, validation, and prioritisation, then clearer, evidence-backed ideation.

Technologies: Ruby on Rails, Stimulus, Tailwind CSS, Azure Container Apps, Azure Pipelines.
