[Super Map World](https://supermap.world/) is a personal project I built after experimenting with generative map styling in D3.js. The site offers thousands of free (and premium) map graphics for 200+ countries and regions—colourful, browsable by location and projection, and editable in the browser, with animation tools for export.

Under the hood it is a Ruby on Rails app (Stimulus, D3, Tailwind) that generates map styles programmatically—randomising projections, palettes, boundaries, and graticules, with contrast correction so land and sea stay legible. What began as a single-page demo on [Creating with Data](https://creatingwithdata.com/) grew into a full product launch.
