I created the [Open Engagement Checklist](https://github.com/digitalWestie/open-engagement) after delivering a workshop on “Achieving Engagement & Impact with Open Data”. This is an attempt to distill lessons learned from working in and around open data initiatives. You can read the [checklist here](https://github.com/digitalWestie/open-engagement/blob/master/OE-BEST-PRACTICE.md) where you'll find a list of practical steps to make your open data initiative more engaging and impactful.

Topics include making data findable, clear licensing, access and usability, demand-driven publishing, quality metadata, context for datasets, supporting conversation around data, building capacity, and collaborating on data as a common resource. The document adapts earlier work by Opquast, Tim Davies, and others.
