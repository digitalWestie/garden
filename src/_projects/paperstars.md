Led full-stack development of [Paperstars](https://paperstars.org/) with product studio [Lumi](https://www.lumi.studio/). Paperstars is a platform where researchers find, rate, and review published papers on scientific merit: methods, statistics, data presentation, limitations, and transparency, rather than journal prestige or citations.

Reviews are [anonymous by default](https://paperstars.org/about) so people can give honest, critical feedback without career risk. Academics can verify with an institutional email (badge on profile) while remaining unnamed on reviews; when you engage with a paper you get a one-off pseudonym (e.g. “PurpleMicroscope”) for that paper only. Authors can claim papers and respond; moderation keeps discourse professional.

The goal is a lasting quality signal alongside traditional citations so good science is easier to find and weak work is visible to the community building on it.
