[ALISS](https://www.alliance-scotland.org.uk/digital/aliss-a-local-information-system-for-scotland-2/what-is-aliss/) (A Local Information System for Scotland) helps people find and share information on health and wellbeing resources, services, and support in their local community and online. It is a national digital programme from the Health and Social Care Alliance Scotland (the ALLIANCE), co-designed so communities can contribute information themselves.

I did not build ALISS from scratch. I **took over and stewarded** the existing platform for about two years: kept it running in production, supported the internal ALLIANCE team, and made sure the system worked for external organisations contributing and maintaining crowdsourced local service data.

During that time I also introduced a number of UI improvements and **led delivery of a new API version** based on the [OpenReferral](https://openreferral.org/) data standard.

Technologies: Python, Django, Heroku, Elasticsearch, jQuery, OpenReferral.
