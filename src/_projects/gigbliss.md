Technical implementation for [GigBliss](https://entretags.de/gigbliss/)-a series of connected hairdryer prototypes (Plus, Balance, Auto) that explore how smart contracts on Ethereum could mediate distributed energy trading and different levels of user control. Led by Larissa Pschetz with Chris Speed; commissioned for [#Blockchain4EU](https://entretags.de/gigbliss/) at the EU Policy Lab. See the [project video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTA1ZDFINiI) and the broader Design Informatics portfolio in [*Blockchain and Beyond*](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3503462) (ACM TOCHI).
