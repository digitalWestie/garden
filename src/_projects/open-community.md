Consulted with Snook and council partners on the [Open Community](https://opencommunity.org.uk/about/) discovery-reviewing existing standards (OpenReferral, Locally Delivered Services, Open Eligibility) and drafting a framework to evaluate them on technical and governance grounds, as [week notes #1](https://opencommunity.org.uk/week-notes-1/) describes. Findings and recommendations are in the [discovery report on data standards (PDF)](https://opencommunity.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Report-OpenCommunity-Data-standards.pdf). That work informed the [Open Referral UK](https://openreferraluk.org/) standard and the later [Local Digital beta](https://www.localdigital.gov.uk/funded-project/open-community-beta/).
