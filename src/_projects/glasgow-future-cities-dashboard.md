Developed the front-end for the [Future Cities Dashboard](https://nigeljohnwade.design/glasgow-smart-cities) as part of Glasgow’s £24m [Future Cities Demonstrator](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/24-million-investment-will-make-glasgow-a-city-of-the-future) - turning a graphic design and back-end APIs into a working interface that consumed live city data and presented it in novel, explorable views.

The dashboard sat within the wider [OPEN Glasgow](https://urbantide.com/output/open-glasgow) programme (data, innovation, and engagement workstreams), which helped open up council and city systems and was later cited as a flagship of the city’s [smart-city investment](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/glasgow-becomes-a-world-leading-smart-city).
